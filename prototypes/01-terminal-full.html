§ 04 / DOCTRINE

How we operate

Operating principles, non-negotiable.

A short doctrine.
Mostly what we refuse to do.

¶ 001

We take operational ownership, not advisory tickets.

If a system doesn't run, the engagement isn't done. We name the operator, set the gate, ship the change, and stay on the on-call rotation until the metric holds.

¶ 002

Evidence is a byproduct, not a deliverable.

Audit trails, control surfaces, and regulatory artifacts are produced as the system runs — not assembled in a sprint after the fact. The work and the proof are the same artifact.

¶ 003

We refuse the slide deck deliverable.

A working pipeline, a control gate, a runbook owned by a named person. The artifact is the system. If we hand back a deck, we have failed.
