§ 01 / WHEN THIS FIRES

Symptoms in the operating surface

If any of these hold, this is your mandate.

Self-diagnostic.
Not exhaustive.

01
Exception queues are growing faster than ops headcount. Manual triage absorbs analyst hours; the queue gets older, not smaller.
02
Straight-through rate has plateaued. STP per rail (SEPA, FPS, FedNow, RTP, ACH) is stuck despite incremental fixes.
03
High-value events get the same attention as low-value ones. Prioritisation by financial impact isn't built into the workflow.
04
Leadership lacks cycle-time and loss visibility. Backlog and preventable-loss drivers aren't reported in real time.
05
Cross-border and on/off-ramp flows leak time. SWIFT gpi, fiat-to-digital-asset routes show settlement-confidence gaps.
06
Off-chain controls aren't integrated into decisions. KYC/KYB, sanctions, ledger checks live outside the decision flow.
