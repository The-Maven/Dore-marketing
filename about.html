Who builds the agents

Anthony Kwawu.

Founder, Rayleigh Stark.  Always-on agents for stablecoin operations.

Money is becoming programmable. Stablecoins are the rail it travels on — regulated, transparent, settling 24/7 alongside the agents that will increasingly do the deciding inside institutional workflows. The economic plumbing of the next decade is being laid right now, by people building it and people governing it. I'm here to help build the operations layer it needs.

Always-on operations isn't new. The agentic version of it is. Reserves don't go offline. Sanctions screens don't sleep. Cross-chain reconciliation doesn't wait for treasury to come back online on a Tuesday morning. Operations runs at the cadence of the money it watches — and shows its work in evidence a regulator can read on a bank-holiday morning.

Before this, almost a decade inside a global investment bank — LLM analytics, decision systems, and transaction monitoring infrastructure under real institutional load. Policy-bound, audit-ready, deployed where it matters. That's the discipline Rayleigh Stark brings to stablecoin issuers today, through Hermes, our runtime.

Why this work, now

Money has always run on infrastructure.

The dollar. The wire. The bank holiday. Every transaction since has flowed through decisions made decades ago, in rooms most people will never see. Stablecoins are the next decision. Programmable currency, regulated under MiCA and the GENIUS Act, settling around the clock on the chains the agentic economy is being built on. Not a retail-crypto story — a story about how institutions move money, and how machines will move it on their behalf.

Rayleigh Stark builds the operations layer for that money. Agents that watch reserves, redemptions, sanctions, KYT, and cross-chain reconciliation — not because operations is the glamour, but because it's where this future holds together or doesn't. Every regulator-facing event, every threshold approach, every coverage drift: documented, attested, gated. Built so that when the system needs human eyes, the agent has already drafted the packet and called the right name.
