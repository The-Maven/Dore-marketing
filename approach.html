How the agents land

From operations audit, to design partner, to always-on in production.

Two ways in. The artifact is a deployed agent watching reserves, redemptions, or sanctions, not a deck. Hermes runtime, your operational surface, regulator-ready evidence.

01
Operations audit Two weeks

We map your stablecoin operations against the surfaces we cover.

Reserves coverage. Redemption flow. Sanctions and KYT. Cross-chain reconciliation. Regulatory reporting. We walk the surfaces with your treasury, ops, and compliance leads, and look at what is already watched and where the picture goes dark.

The output is a deployment plan with measurable targets and the surfaces where always-on coverage adds the most value. Bridges into a design partner engagement or stands alone.

02
Design partner Six to eight weeks to first production

We deploy always-on agents inside one operational surface.

For stablecoin issuers and infrastructure firms running production today. The agents run on Hermes, our runtime, governed by your policy, integrated with your custody feeds, ledger, sanctions screening, and ticketing.

The agents ship into production with measurable improvement in coverage, time to escalation, or evidence-ready output, with a named operator on your side accountable for them.

03
Live operation Subscription thereafter

Policy versioned. Kill switches in place. Evidence ready.

Dual approval gates on policy changes. Policy versioned. Kill switches configured for the head of compliance and the CFO. Evidence retention configured per MiCA, the GENIUS Act, and equivalent regimes.

Monthly subscription covers the runtime, policy reviews, and human escalation on edge cases. The agents stay deployed. Your team owns the surface.

What we refuse

The artifact is the agent. Not a deck.

We do not hand back a slide deck. We do not write a strategy paper. We do not run a multi-year programme.

If the agents are not watching reserves, redemptions, or sanctions better than your team can on day one, the deployment failed.
